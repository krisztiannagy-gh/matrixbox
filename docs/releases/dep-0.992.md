### Passenger announcements

A new **Passenger announcements** card on the settings page, shown while the BKK FUTÁR skin is active. Needs BKK skin v8, which installs automatically.

- **Full-screen PA:** replaces the departures. Each row is fixed text (input stops at the board's edge) or scrolling text, with a preview of the board. Start immediately or at a date and time; end never or at a date and time.
- **Scrolling PA:** one text (up to 250 characters) looping in the bottom row, with the same start and end options. A full-screen PA hides it; it comes back afterwards if it's still live.
- **Reminder:** a bottom-row text that comes back every 2–60 minutes for 1–5 loops. It's skipped while another PA is on the board.
- Separator per PA (default `***`, empty = a gap), scroll speed slow / normal / fast.
- A PA is locked while it's on. Switching it off, or reaching its end, lets the current pass finish first. PAs that reach their end switch themselves off.
- The card shows the **box time**, which start and end times use.

Update from the box's **Apps** page (restart the box first so it sees the new version).
