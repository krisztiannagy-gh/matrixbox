### London Underground skin

- **New skin: London Underground.** Pick it as the skin on the settings page; London Underground skin v1 downloads by itself.
- Its settings block has:
  - *Long station names*: scroll or cut.
  - *Hide trains to*: destinations of the other direction, e.g. `Morden, Kennington`.
  - *Line* and *Direction*: shown when there are no trains.
- The station's *Direction* dropdown is hidden while this skin is active. T-Skylt sends no direction for London, so it does nothing there.
- Skins can now get the seconds to each train's expected time. The London skin uses them to time when a train leaves the board. Other skins are unchanged.

Update from the box's **Apps** page (restart the box first so it sees the new version).
